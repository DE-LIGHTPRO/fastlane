Pilot proposal

A shorter checkout queue on weekend nights

A proposal for a one-branch pilot of FastLane at bokku! Mart.

The problem

On weekend evenings the checkout queue at a busy branch can take 30 minutes or more. We have stood in it. The cause is simple: every item in every basket is scanned at the till, one at a time, while a long line of shoppers waits with nothing to do.

The idea

Let shoppers scan their own basket on their phone while they wait. At a fast lane, a checker scans one code, confirms the basket with a guided spot check, and takes payment the usual way: cash, POS or transfer. Shoppers without a phone can have their basket scanned in the queue by a member of staff.

Why this is different

  • It starts in the queue, when shoppers have a reason to try it and can see the fast lane moving.
  • Nothing to install: it opens in the phone browser from a printed sign.
  • Payment does not change, so nobody has to trust a new payment app.
  • It is optional. The normal tills stay open.

The pilot

  • Where: one branch with a known weekend queue.
  • When: Friday to Sunday evenings, for four weekends.
  • How: one or two existing tills become fast lanes, with a sign where the queue forms.

What we need from the store

  • An export of the product list: barcodes, names and prices.
  • One checker per fast lane, using a phone with a camera.
  • Mobile signal or shopper Wi-Fi in the queue area.
  • A short briefing with the branch team before the first night.

What we provide

  • The software, set up with the store product list.
  • The printed queue signs.
  • Training for the checkers, and support on site on pilot nights.
  • A report after each weekend.

How losses are controlled

  • Prices come from the store list, never from the shopper phone.
  • The checker must find named items, always including the most expensive one, and count the basket.
  • Baskets over 25 items are fully counted.
  • Each pass works once and expires after 15 minutes.
  • A sample of fast-lane baskets is fully re-scanned each night to measure losses.

What we will measure

  • Time in the queue, before and during the pilot.
  • The share of shoppers who choose the fast lane.
  • Shoppers served per till, per hour.
  • The mismatch rate found by the nightly audits.
  • What checkers and shoppers say about it.

Before the first night

The demo at the address below runs without a server. Before a live pilot we will add a shared server, so every fast lane sees the same record of used passes, load the store product list, and agree how sales are reported back to the till system.

Cost

There is no charge for the pilot. If the results are good, pricing for a wider rollout is agreed afterwards.
