Privacy

What this demo does with your camera and your data.

  • The camera only reads codesPictures from your camera are read on your phone to find a barcode or a pass. No photo or video is saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your basket stays on your phoneBaskets, orders and sales records are kept in this browser on this device. This demo has no server, so nothing is uploaded.
  • No account, no trackingFastLane does not ask for your name, phone number or email. It sets no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • What a pass containsA pass holds a basket number, the time it was made and the barcodes and quantities in the basket. It holds nothing about you.
  • Clearing your dataTap "Start again" on the shopper page, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

A live service that takes payment or stores orders on a server would need a fuller notice. This page describes the demo as it is today.
